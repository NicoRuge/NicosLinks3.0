Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is valid for https://files.nico-ruge.de.

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation is the person identified in the Imprint.

2. Hosting and Operation (Nextcloud)

Under the subdomain files.nico-ruge.de, I operate a self-hosted Nextcloud instance hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. A data processing agreement has been concluded with the hosting provider.
When using the service, technical connection data (for example IP address, date and time of access, requested resource, user agent) may be processed to ensure secure and stable operation.
Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

3. Photographs at Political and Association Events

I take photographs at political party and association events for documentation purposes. Participants may be identifiable in these images.
Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in documenting the event and providing the images to participants. Where required, processing is carried out on the basis of consent.
Images are made available exclusively via password-protected download links. No public gallery or search engine indexing takes place.
The images are stored only for as long as necessary for the intended purpose.

4. File Upload via Protected Share Links

In individual cases, files can be transmitted via protected share links. Uploaded content as well as technical connection data such as IP address and time of transmission are processed.
Processing is carried out solely for the purpose of receiving and handling the transmitted files. Data is not shared with third parties unless explicitly agreed.
The data is deleted once the respective purpose no longer applies and no statutory retention obligations exist.

5. Rights of Data Subjects

Data subjects have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability within the framework of applicable law.
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

6. Changes

This privacy policy is effective as of February 2026. Amendments may be made if legal requirements or the offered services change.
